# ACMP · Butterfly Effect Model, in 30 seconds

**1 actor · 1 action = consequences in cascade.** · [Full README](README.md)

**WHY.** AI agents are starting to act, but language models predict the next word, not what an action changes.

| Action | LLM prediction | ACMP prediction |
|---|---|---|
| Lightning strikes an AI data center | “Backup generators should take over.” | Physical → Digital → Business → Media → Earth: the chain across layers, with its unknowns listed |
| A layer with nothing believable to add | Fills it with something plausible | “No consequence found” |

**WHAT.** The extension of our previous model, [ACM](https://github.com/Sherlock2019/ACM), which predicts the
consequences of an AI's action in its digital world. ACMP takes this to a ten-layer world view: Earth,
Government, Society & Economy, Business, Media, Network, Digital, Behaviour, Perception, and Physical (down
to quantum). It is a proof of concept that runs on prototype rules and authored consequences. It is not a
trained model.

**SO WHAT.** Second-order effects in seconds, every step explained, and never an invented probability. Built
for risk, infrastructure, policy, communications and AI-safety teams *(target users still to be validated)*.

**HOW.** A router picks the layer the action hits first. The cascade then spreads through rules, and each
link is marked EFFECT, NO EFFECT, UNKNOWN or NOT MODELED. Every scenario plays in 3D (Babylon.js + Havok).
22 scenarios, 49 tests, fully offline.
